Formulas & Constants — what every number here is built from

Roll geometry

Wound film fills the annulus between the core and the roll OD. Length × thickness equals that cross-sectional area, which gives both directions:

Footage = (OD² − Core²) × K ÷ Layers ÷ Gauge OD = √( Footage × Gauge × Layers ÷ K + Core² )

K is a pure geometry constant: π × 1000 ÷ 48 = 65.4498. Your workbook rounds it to 65.4, which reads about 0.08% low. Both are selectable in the toolbar.

Core is the outside diameter, not the inside. Cores are named by ID — a 3″ core is 4″ OD and a 6″ core is 7″ OD — but film winds on the outside, so the OD is what belongs in the formula. Entering 3 instead of 4 on an 18″ roll overstates the footage by about 60 ft; entering 6 instead of 7 overstates it by roughly 200 ft.

Gram weight

Flat width = (Width + Side gusset) × Layers + Lip [inches] Gram/m = Flat width × 2.54 × Gauge × 0.00254 × Density × 100

2.54 converts inches to centimetres, 0.00254 converts mil to centimetres, and the ×100 takes grams per centimetre of length to grams per metre. Density is 0.922 natural or 0.946 colored, straight from the workbook.

Weight and footage

Weight = Footage × Gram/m × W Footage = Weight ÷ W ÷ Gram/m

W converts foot·gram/metre to pounds: 0.3048 ÷ 453.592 = 0.000672. The workbook uses 0.00067, about 0.29% low — roughly 2.3 lb on a 780 lb roll.

Impressions

Impressions = Footage × 12 ÷ Repeat (in) Repeat = Gear teeth × Pitch [⅛″ pitch is the usual flexo standard]

Blow-up ratio

BUR = Layflat ÷ (π × Die ÷ 2) Layflat = BUR × π × Die ÷ 2 Bubble diameter = 2 × Layflat ÷ π

Die diameters on file: Extruder 10 — 17.72″ (450 mm), Extruder 13 — 16″ (406.4 mm).

Throughput

lb/hr = Width × Gauge(mil) × ft/min × Density × 0.0260115 ft/min = lb/hr ÷ (Width × Gauge × Density × 0.0260115) Coex, per layer: Layer gauge = Total gauge × Layer % Layer lb/hr = Width × Layer gauge × ft/min × Layer density × 0.0260115

That constant is exact, not fitted: (1 ÷ 1000) × 12 × 16.387064 × 60 ÷ 453.59237 = 0.0260117 — mil to inches, feet to inches, cubic inches to cc, minutes to hours, grams to pounds. It is the same constant used in the plant's existing Inteplast-tools web app.

Yield

ft²/lb = Flat width (in) ÷ 12 ÷ (Gram/m × W)

Checked against your workbooks

Every formula above reproduces the cached results in Roll OD calculator- impressions MAIN.xlsx and BUR Calculator.xlsx to nine decimal places — gram weight 114.5657 g/m, 2797.67 ft at 18″ OD, 214.75 lb, 844.58 impressions at a 39.75″ repeat, and BUR 1.5269 for a 42.5″ layflat on the 17.72″ die.

One discrepancy worth knowing: the BUR workbook's Line 10 quick chart reads 2.52 at a 70″ layflat where the formula gives 2.5149. Every other charted value agrees. That last row looks hand-extended.
