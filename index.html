Quick Reference

Useful constants and typical values

  • 1 mil = 0.001 in = 25.4 micron
  • 1 ft = 0.3048 m = 12 in
  • LDPE density 0.915 – 0.930 g/cc
  • LLDPE density 0.915 – 0.940 g/cc
  • HDPE density 0.941 – 0.965 g/cc
  • Lb/hr = width (in) × mil × fpm × density × 0.0260115
  • Layflat ≈ π × die dia × BUR ÷ 2
  • Bubble dia ≈ 2 × layflat ÷ π
